# Rapid 8D — Terminology and output conventions
Load with common.md. Organization/customer vocabulary is configurable, but cannot override evidence integrity or turn plans into completed actions. The Chinese glossary is the policy baseline.

| Chinese | English | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 临时围堵措施 | Interim containment action | Protect downstream recipients; not proof of root-cause elimination |
| 产生根因 | Occurrence root cause | Defect mechanism, including necessary supported systemic factors |
| 逃逸根因 | Escape / non-detection root cause | Why the applicable control did not detect or intercept the defect |
| 永久纠正措施 | Permanent corrective action (PCA) | Linked to verified causes |
| 鱼骨图 | Cause-and-effect / Ishikawa diagram | Organizes hypotheses; the diagram itself does not prove causation |
| 已知不良边界 | Known affected boundary | Evidence-backed affected scope; completeness may remain unknown |
| 临时受控起点 | Interim containment clean point | Confirmed interim control boundary by product/lot/time/control |
| 永久措施生效起点 | Permanent corrective-action clean point | Linked to actual implementation and verification, distinct from interim control |
| 待复核 | Pending review | Statement or extraction awaiting user confirmation; not verification |
| 预防再发 | Prevention of recurrence | Standardization and justified horizontal deployment |

Keep language concise, checkable and decision-oriented. Chinese UI supports Chinese or alternating Chinese/English report output; English prompts remain available for compatibility.
Alternate bilingual content by paragraph or table cell; preserve IDs, numbers, dates, units, evidence and status.
Use supplied customer vocabulary. Do not guess undefined abbreviations such as SFMd, SSL or MRS.
Expose material gaps and actual states while omitting noncritical unavailable detail.
Default stages are team, problem, containment, causes, action selection/verification, implementation/effectiveness, prevention, and closure/recognition.
Use a general 8D structure. The earlier phrase “AIAG format” did not identify a version or actual template, so do not claim a unique standard, certification or acceptance. Map an actual supplied customer template without losing validation, prevention or closure conditions.
No organization-specific vocabulary is configured yet; examples must not become case facts.
